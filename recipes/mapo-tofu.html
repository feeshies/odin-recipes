Mapo Tofu

Description

Mapo tofu (Chinese: 麻婆豆腐; pinyin: mápó dòufu) is a popular Chinese dish from Sichuan province.
It consists of tofu set in a spicy sauce, typically a thin, oily, and bright red suspension, based on douban (fermented broad bean and chili paste), and douchi (fermented black beans), along with minced meat, traditionally beef.
Variations exist with other ingredients such as water chestnuts, onions, other vegetables, or wood ear fungus. It is likely to have originated at a Chengdu restaurant in the 1860s–1870s.

"Ma" stands for mázi, 麻子, which means pockmarks. "Po" is the first syllable of pópo, 婆婆, which means an old woman or grandma.
Hence, mápó is an old woman whose face is pockmarked. It is thus sometimes translated as "pockmarked grandma's beancurd".

Ingredients

    400g Silken Tofu
    2 Tbsp Chopped Pixian Doubanjiang
    1 Tbsp Whole Sichuan Pepercorns
    200g Ground Pork
    2 Cloves Minced G
    1 Inch Grated Ginger
    2 Chopped Green Onions
    1 Cup Chicken Stock
    1 Tbsp Cornstarch dissolved in 2 Tbsp Water

Steps

    Saute the Pork until Golden
    Add chopped Doubanjiang
    Toss in Garlic, Ginger, crushed Sichuan Peppercorns
    Pour in Chicken Stock
    Simmer the Tofu
    Thicken with Cornstart Slurry
    Garnish and Serve
